# The manager GUI's fonts

Bitmap fonts in LVGL's own format, generated by `tools/make_fonts.py`; do not
edit the `.c` files. Three sizes each (14 px lines, 18 px items, 24 px titles),
4 bits per pixel:

| Font | Characters | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nbt_font_latin_<size>` | Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, common punctuation; the names 中文 and 日本語 | Montserrat Medium (the face of LVGL's built-in fonts), with its kerning; the two names from Noto Sans CJK JP |
| `nbt_font_sc_<size>` | The characters of `src/gui/text/zh.txt` outside the Latin font | Noto Sans CJK SC Regular |
| `nbt_font_jp_<size>` | The characters of `src/gui/text/ja.txt` outside the Latin font | Noto Sans CJK JP Regular |

The Chinese and Japanese fonts fall back to the Latin font of the same size and
share its line height and baseline. `glyphs.json` records the character sets
and the source files' SHA-256. `tests/test_gui_text.py` checks that every
character of every catalog is in a font.

**After changing a Chinese or Japanese text**, regenerate:

```sh
python3 tools/make_fonts.py --montserrat Montserrat-Medium.ttf \
  --cjk /usr/share/fonts/opentype/noto/NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc
```

`Montserrat-Medium.ttf` (version 9.000) is in
https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat (`fonts/ttf`);
`NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc` (version 2.004) in https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
(`Sans/OTC`) or a distribution's `fonts-noto-cjk`. The generator needs
fontTools and Pillow with FreeType.
