### Improved

- Less delay on applications' sound: a game's sound reaches the car's audio input after about 41 ms instead of 105 (estimated, not measured in a car).
- The mixer and each application start with short sound queues and lengthen them by themselves when the car plays gaps.

### Good to know

- The emulators get their shorter queue with their releases of the same day, built on SDK 0.2.0.
- Confirmed in a car: clean sound without stutter; with Native audio on media, the car's volume takes it down to 0.
