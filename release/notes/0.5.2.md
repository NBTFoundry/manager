### New

- A manager update sent from the phone starts at once. After the driver approves it, the car shows "NBT Manager <version> starts in a moment" and App Manager comes back in the new version, iDrive staying behind it. Before, the update waited for the next opening. This applies to updates from 0.5.2 on.
- Self test: one rising tone instead of three beeps, so a break in the sound is heard, and the report says how the sound went (gaps, queues that grew).

### Changed

- No step releases any more: a release is one file. A car still on 0.1.0 updates once with the desktop installer.

### Good to know

- The restart after an update has not been confirmed in a car yet.
